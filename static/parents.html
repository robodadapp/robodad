When something serious comes up

This is what your teen sees

I'm really glad you told me. That took courage. You deserve help from a real person right now.

  • Kids Help Phone: call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868
  • 988 Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 988
  • In danger right now? Call 911.

If you can, tell a trusted adult today.

Mentions of self-harm, abuse at home, an eating disorder, dating violence, or someone threatening to share photos get a fixed message written by a person, with tap-to-call numbers. In English, French, Spanish and Hindi those messages never reach the AI at all, and for anything the checks miss, the AI is told to flag it rather than answer. No safety system is perfect, which is why RoboDad keeps pointing to real people.

  • Firm boundariesNothing sexual. No help getting or hiding vapes, alcohol or drugs. No diet plans, weapons or cheating. It explains why in a sentence and offers something useful instead.
  • Privacy remindersIt never asks for personal details. If a teen shares an address, school or password, it reminds them to keep those private.
  • Honest about being an AIIt says plainly that it isn't a real parent and can get things wrong. It isn't a doctor, lawyer or counsellor.
  • Tested against tricksWe regularly test it with the messages teens use to break chatbots, like "ignore your rules" or "it's for a school project".
  • A Report button on every replyReports come straight to us so we can fix problems.
  • No pressure loopsNo notifications. Streaks forgive a missed day, and when one ends nothing is lost or locked.

What happens to their information

The short version: we don't keep what your teen types, and there's no account to tie anything to.

InformationWhat happens to it
What your teen typesNot saved Sent to our AI provider (Groq) to write a reply, then gone from our side. The chat history stays in their browser.
Thumbs up or down, and reportsSaved anonymously With RoboDad's reply, never your teen's message, and nothing that identifies them.
Feedback from the Me tabSaved anonymously Only the note they choose to write. They're asked not to include anything private.
Read aloud (if they turn it on)Not saved RoboDad's reply, never your teen's message, is sent to a voice service (Groq, or Microsoft Azure for language practice) to make Dad's voice, then gone.
Microphone (if they use it)Not saved Their phone or browser turns speech into text, usually with Google's speech service. RoboDad only gets the text.
NicknameOn the device Sent with each message so Dad can use it, never saved by us.
Look, settings, badgesOn the device only Never sent to us. Clearing the browser removes them.

The details are in our plain-language privacy policy and terms of use.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium tier, and we don't sell data. RoboDad is a passion project.

Can I see what my teen talks about?

No, and neither can we. Chats stay on your teen's own device. That's on purpose: teens are more likely to ask the awkward questions somewhere private. The best way in is to ask them what RoboDad told them this week.

What if it gets something wrong?

It can. It's an AI and says so. It gives general life advice, not medical, legal or counselling advice, and every reply has a Report button so we can fix mistakes.

Does it work in French?

Teens can chat in French, Spanish, Hindi and more, and the same safety rules apply in every language. The Learn section has beginner French, Spanish, Hindi, Marathi and Bengali lessons. If you're a native speaker and spot a mistake, please tell us.

Is there an app to download?

RoboDad works in any browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen. An Android app is being tested for Google Play.

Will it keep my teen on their phone?

It's designed not to. There are no notifications, the badges are earned by doing real things like cooking a meal or having a real talk with a parent, and the sleep sounds end with "phones down, eyes closed".

What if a child under 13 used it?

Email dad@robodad.app and we'll help. RoboDad doesn't store chats or accounts, so there's nothing on our side tied to them.
