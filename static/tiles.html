Words played (tap a word and Dad explains it)

    Pick your dad

    Three ways to place tiles: drag a tile onto a square; or tap a tile, then a square; or tap an empty square first (tap it again to switch ▶ across / ▼ down) and then tap your letters in order. Drag tiles along your rack to reorder them, or hit 🔀 to shuffle and spot new words. Tap a tile on the board to take it back. All your tiles go in one line, across or down, and join up with what's already there. The first word covers the star. Use all seven tiles in one go for a Full House: 50 extra points.

    The clock: your thinking time is capped for the whole game, and the tougher the dad, the less you get (15 minutes against Tier 1 down to 6 against the boss). It only runs on your turn. If it hits zero, the game ends and the higher score wins. Tap the clock to turn it off for a relaxed game.

    Bonuses: play a Glow-Up word (a classier word for an everyday one) for +10, and the day's Power Word for double points. Cover a ✦ Simile Square, play a verb, or every few turns, and Dad gives you a quick bonus round: a simile, a Tense Twist or a sentence.
