RoboDad Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 (new web addresses, maths, anonymous link counts and votes)

RoboDad is an AI chatbot that gives teenagers practical advice about everyday life. It is a passion project made by Saikat Sunil Ganguly in Toronto, Canada. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it. It covers robodad.app (the app), robodad.ca (the page for parents) and robodad.online (which forwards to the Learn with Dad page on robodad.app).

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and RoboDad never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your messages are sent to an AI service to write replies. RoboDad does not save your messages.
  • Your chat and nickname stay in your own browser on your device.
  • Secret codes you type in the menu are checked by our server and never saved. What a code unlocks is remembered only on your device.
  • If you rate or report a reply, we save the rating and RoboDad's reply, never what you typed.
  • No ads, no selling of data and no tracking cookies.

Parents: there's a two-minute overview at RoboDad for parents.

Who RoboDad is for

RoboDad is meant for people aged 13 and older. It asks for your birth year the first time you open it and only remembers whether you passed the age check, not the year itself. It is not directed at children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used RoboDad, contact us and we'll help.

Voice features

If you tap the microphone, your browser's own speech recognition turns your voice into text. In some browsers, such as Chrome, the audio is processed by the browser maker's speech service. RoboDad only receives the text. "Read aloud" uses your device's built-in voice, so nothing extra is sent anywhere.

Services RoboDad uses

  • Render hosts the website (United States). Render uses Cloudflare to protect and deliver it.
  • Groq runs the AI model that writes replies (United States).
  • Supabase stores ratings, reports, tester sign-ups, link counts and votes (United States).
  • Porkbun is where the web addresses are registered, and it forwards robodad.online to robodad.app.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface, so your browser contacts Google to load it.

Because these services are in the United States, information may be processed there.

Keeping yourself safe

  • Don't share your full name, address, school, passwords or photos with RoboDad or any chatbot.
  • RoboDad is an AI. It can be wrong, and it isn't a replacement for a parent, doctor or counsellor.
  • If you're in danger, call 911. Kids Help Phone is available any time at 1-800-668-6868 or by texting CONNECT to 686868.

Your choices

  • Tap New chat to clear the conversation from your screen.
  • Remove your nickname with the person icon, then Don't use a name.
  • Ratings and reports aren't linked to you, so we can't look up ones you sent. You can still contact us with any question.

Deleting your data

RoboDad (the website and the RoboDad app on Google Play) keeps almost nothing about you, so deleting it is quick:

  • Your chats: tap New chat. They're only stored in your browser or app on your device, and we never had a copy.
  • Nickname, look, settings, badges and learning progress: clear your browser's data for robodad.app, or in the Android app go to Settings, then Apps, then RoboDad, then Storage, then Clear storage. Uninstalling the app also removes them.
  • Tester sign-up email: email dad@robodad.app from that address with the subject "Delete my RoboDad data". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Otherwise it's deleted automatically when Play Store testing ends.
  • Ratings, reports, link counts and votes: these are anonymous and never linked to you, so we can't find "your" ones to delete. They contain RoboDad's replies or a platform or topic name, never what you typed.
