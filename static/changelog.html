What's new

Pull up a chair, kiddo. RoboDad started out as a little chatbot living in a terminal window, and look at him now. Here's every change, newest first. And no, you can't skip to the end.

  1. Version 1.24 · November 2026Latest

    Grammar with Dad

    "Yesterday, a confused pigeon juggled the Wi-Fi password." Grammatically perfect. Otherwise, a mystery.

    • Simile, Metaphor, Analogy: spin the Comparison Machine to turn silly things into a simile ("as loud as a jet engine") and a metaphor ("is a jet engine"), then learn what makes an analogy different: it explains, not just describes.
    • Two games: Simile, metaphor or analogy? and Finish the analogy ("Puppy is to dog as kitten is to…").
    • Tense Trouble: one tiny tense change, a totally different meaning. "I ate the cake" is a confession. "I will eat the cake" is a threat.
    • Silly Sentence Time Machine: Dad spins a nonsense sentence and you send it to the past, the future, right now, or turn it into a question. Watch out for sneaky verbs like ate, threw and brought.
    • A time map of six tenses, XP and streaks, and an Ask Dad button on every answer.
    • 13 new badges for Vocabulary and Grammar: Glow Getter, Mic Drop, Word Nerd, Flawless Victory, Top of the Class, Cool as a Cucumber, Reel Talk, Eagle Eye, Analogy Ace, Cake Detective, Time Traveller, Grammar Guru… and one secret one for whoever out-words Robo-Grandpa.
  2. Version 1.23 · November 2026

    Vocabulary with Dad

    "Thy argument is as hollow as a drum, and twice as loud." Dad's been waiting years to say that.

    • Sentence Glow-Up: paste a real sentence, like an email to a teacher or a message to your coach, and Dad polishes it and shows you the one or two changes that made the difference. Eight worked examples work even offline.
    • Classy Comebacks: witty, clean lines for weak arguments, braggers, lost games and friendly roasts, plus Shakespeare mode. Every one is aimed at the idea, never the person. Share your favourites.
    • Word Origins with Dad: a one-minute word story every day. Why "clue" was once a ball of thread, why "robot" is Czech, and a couple of famous word myths, busted.
    • Vocab Duel: a fast eight-question match game with a timer, streaks and XP. Climb the chess ladder from Modern Dad to Professor Dad. Beat him and a secret challenger appears.
    • The Lost Cartridge now has a Word clue in every chapter. Learning words pays off in the hunt.
  3. Version 1.22 · November 2026

    Talk to your folks: Marathi and Bengali

    "Say 'jevlis ka?' to your Aai once. Then watch the second helping arrive."

    • Marathi (मराठी) and Bengali (বাংলা) are here, next to Hindi. Eight lessons each, built for home: saying you're home, food talk, the right words for every relative, talking to elders with respect, feelings, and festival wishes.
    • Five family role-plays per language: come home to Aai or Ma, take a phone call from Aaji or Dadu, visit relatives at Diwali or Durga Puja, and more.
    • Eight new practice badges, from Pahila Paul to Lay Bhari and Prothom Dhap to Darun! They count towards Globe Trotter too.
    • Fluent speakers are still checking these lessons. Spot something off? Tell me with the thumbs-down, and ask your folks which words your family uses.
    • Want Tulu, Telugu or another family language? Vote for it on the Learn page.
  4. Version 1.21 · November 2026

    New voices and a few secrets

    "I'm not saying there's a secret code. I'm just saying the 80s had some great ones."

    • My four voices have new names: Joe King, Ray Dio, Al Gorithm and Sir Kit Breaker. Same voices, much better puns.
    • There's now a "Got a secret code?" box in the ☰ menu. I won't tell you the codes. But if you grew up on arcade games, you might already know one.
    • 🌙 Sleep Sounds. Rain, heavy rain, ocean waves, a stream, wind, a fan, and brown, pink and white noise. Mix as many as you like, each with its own volume. They fade out after 15 minutes, 30 minutes or an hour, or you can turn the timer off. Tap Lights out for a dark screen, then lock your phone and they keep playing. Late at night the home screen asks if you can't sleep. Find it any time in the dashboard, under RoboDad Radio.
    • The Secret Vault. See the treasure chest next to "What's on your mind?" on the home screen? Open it. It shows every secret I've hidden, a clue for each one, and how many you've found. No spoilers, just clues.
    • Safer replies when someone touches you or pulls at your clothes in a way that isn't OK: you now get a clear "that's not OK, and it's not your fault" plus real people to talk to.
    • I understand more ways of asking about privacy, like "are you saving this?" or "can my teacher see this?", so you get a straight answer about where your chats go.
    • Feeling lucky? Tap the decoration in the top-right corner of the home screen (the rangoli at Diwali) or the 🎲 in the same spot, and I'll surprise you with a new look, a new dad and a new tune. Don't like it? Tap again. I've got outfits for days.
  5. Version 1.20 · October 2026

    Study Hall, Teacher Dads, maths and Spanish

    "¿Dónde está el baño? Most useful sentence in any language. You're welcome."

    • Study Hall: a focus timer for homework and exams. Pick 25/5, 15/3 or 45/10, put your phone face down, and I'll keep time with a brand-new lo-fi station. A little timer pill follows you around the app, the screen stays on, and every round earns you closer to the First Focus, Study Buddy and Ten-Hour Club badges. No streaks. Breaks are mandatory. Dad's orders.
    • Teacher Dads: four new looks in the ☰ menu. Chalkboard with Professor Dad (tweed, bow tie, pop quizzes), Science Lab with Lab Dad (goggles, bubbling flasks, the occasional small explosion), Library with Bookworm Dad (cardigan, Earl Grey, "Shh!") and Gym Class with Coach Dad (whistle, clipboard, new PBs). Professor Dad now runs the maths class and Bookworm Dad the language lessons.
    • Maths with Dad for Grades 7–10: twelve topics from integers and fractions to factoring and SOH CAH TOA. Each one has a short explainer, a worked example and a 5-question quiz with a hand-checked answer key. Pass it and you get a maths dad joke. You're welcome.
    • Stuck on homework? Tap Ask Dad for maths coach mode. I give hints and the next step, not the answer, then check your work. That's how it sticks.
    • Four maths badges: Number Cruncher, Equation Ace, Worked It Out and Maths Legend.
    • Spanish joins French and Hindi: eight lessons, quizzes, and role-plays at a taco stand in Mexico City, on the streets of Madrid and in a sneaker shop.
    • Four new Spanish badges, from ¡Hola, Rookie! to Todo el Curso, plus Taco Talker for keeping a role-play going. Globe Trotter now unlocks when you finish any two whole languages.
    • Learn with Dad, a new page at robodad.online with every course and your progress in one place. Vote for the subject or family language you want next.
    • RoboDad has new addresses: robodad.app for the app and robodad.ca for parents.
    • The safety help message now also works in Spanish.
    • New plain-language terms of use, and an updated privacy policy covering the new addresses, maths and anonymous link counts.
    • Fixed: asking "do you save my chats?" no longer offers you a money badge. I now know a privacy question when I see one, and I answer it straight.
  6. Version 1.19

    Practice French and Hindi with Dad

    "Bonjour. Namaste. Now put your shoes away. In any language."

    • Practice with Dad: beginner French and Hindi. Eight short lessons per language, five phrases each, with a speaker button so you can hear them. Find it on the welcome screen or in the dashboard.
    • Every lesson ends with a five-question quiz. Get three right and the lesson's done. Get five and I'd frame it, but you'd say that's embarrassing.
    • Role-plays: order at a café in Montréal, get lost in Québec City, buy chai from a chaiwala, haggle with an auto-rickshaw driver, or survive a chatty uncle asking about your marks. I play the other person, keep it simple, put the English in brackets and give you one gentle "Dad tip" when something's off. Your normal chat waits for you until you tap Exit.
    • Hindi shows up in Devanagari with Roman letters underneath, so you can read it whichever way works. Type in either.
    • Nine new language practice badges, from Bonjour Beginner and Namaste Newbie up to the legendary Globe Trotter. They unlock by themselves as you practise. They mean you've been practising, not that you're fluent. I'm a robot dad, not a language exam.
    • Safety comes first in every language. The help message with real phone numbers now shows up when someone writes about being in danger in French or Hindi (Devanagari or Roman letters), and I can flag it myself in any other language too. We tested it before launch. Twice. That's how dads do it.
  7. Version 1.18

    More holidays, and everything spins

    "Put a sweater on. No, the other sweater."

    • Every look got its own slowly spinning centrepiece: a gear, a neon star, a tape reel, a hubcap, a skateboard wheel, a beach ball, a marigold and more. Watching it is very relaxing. Don't tell your mother I said that.
    • Winter Holidays (Dec 15 to Jan 2): falling snow, a snowy hill and a spinning snowflake. Cozy Dad sips cocoa, throws a snowball and shakes his toque pompom. Station: Snow Day. Player: a snow globe.
    • Lunar New Year (around Feb 6): red and gold, swaying lanterns and a paper-cut medallion. Lucky Dad swings a lantern, hands you a red envelope and sets off firecrackers. Station: Lantern Festival. Player: a swinging lantern.
    • Eid (around Eid al-Fitr in March and Eid al-Adha in May): a crescent moon, gold patterns and a turning geometric star. Eid Dad waves "Eid Mubarak!", lights a lantern and brings out a plate of sweets. Station: Crescent Night. Player: a glowing lantern.
    • Each holiday switches itself on once and steps aside when it's over. If you pick something else, I'll take the hint. Eventually.

  10. Version 1.15

    Talk and thought bubbles

    "We're not running a light show here." So I cleaned the place up.

    • Voice and music now live in two comic bubbles right next to me: a speech bubble for my voice and a thought bubble for the music. That's not going anywhere.
    • Tap a bubble once to switch it on or off. A little note pops up telling you what's playing or who's talking, with a Change button if you want something else. You're welcome.
    • When the music's on, the thought bubble shows your look's own player spinning away, with notes floating out. When I'm reading to you, my speech bubble bounces. Yes, I'm that excited.
    • Sometimes I say a word in my speech bubble when I do a move: "GG!", "Rad!", "Beep!". I've still got it.
    • Only one of me on the screen now. I'm big on the welcome screen, then I glide up into the top bar once we start talking, like a gentleman. Start a new chat and I come right back down.
    • The Voice and Music buttons above the message box are gone. Less clutter. Your room could learn something.
    • Fixed: updates now show up the next time you open me. No more "did you try turning it off and on again?"
    • Fixed: I don't switch voices halfway through a sentence anymore. My online voice gets a free daily allowance, and when it runs out, your phone's voice reads the whole reply. Money doesn't grow on trees, kiddo. My voice is back the next day.
