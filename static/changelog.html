What's new

Pull up a chair, kiddo. RoboDad started out as a little chatbot living in a terminal window, and look at him now. Here's every change, newest first. And no, you can't skip to the end.

  1. Version 1.72 · November 2026

    Sound effects and room music

    "Every click used to be silent. Now the whole house knows you got it wrong."

    • Tile Talk now has wooden tile clicks (four different ones, so it never sounds like a machine gun), a rack shuffle, a soft buzz for a bad word, a score count-up, and a fanfare for a Full House.
    • Quizzes, Vocab Duel and Grammar climb a scale as your streak grows, with a little flourish at 3, 5 and 10 in a row. A wrong answer gets a gentle low sound, never a harsh one.
    • Remote Hunt rustles, and warm or cold guesses sound different. The Prize Counter has ticket and bell sounds.
    • DAD QUEST has a theme for every room, from the Kitchen to the Attic, and every fourth time round the drums drop out so a long run never settles into one loop. Pick "TAPE: ROOM MUSIC" or one of your mixtapes in the menu.
    • Vocab Duel has its own quiet loop for each dad on the ladder, ticking faster in the last two bars. It never cuts in over music you're already playing.
    • The Lost Cartridge hunt has sounds for opening a chapter, a wrong code, each chapter you solve (it gets higher as you go), and a finale when the cartridge boots.
    • Switch them all off under Looks and dads, in "Game sound effects". It remembers your choice.
  2. Version 1.71 · November 2026

    New websites for parents and for learning

    "I put on a clean shirt for this one. Don't get used to it."

    • robodad.ca, the page for parents, is rebuilt: a short demo of how RoboDad hands questions back to parents, real screenshots, what a teen sees when something serious comes up, a plain table of what happens to their information, and answers to the questions parents ask.
    • robodad.online (Learn with Dad) is rebuilt around a chalkboard with the teacher dads, and now shows everything you can learn: five languages, maths, grammar and vocabulary games, chess, Tile Talk, brain teasers, Brain Gym and Study Hall.
    • Links from those pages open the right game in the app straight away.
  3. Version 1.70 · November 2026

    Hands-on, round two: beat Dad, every day

    "I set the scores. You knock them down. That's how dads and kids have always worked."

    • Beat Dad: every Hands-on game now has a dad to beat, with his score shown up top. Beat him and the next dad steps up, all the way to Robo-Grandpa. The trash talk is about your moves, never you, and you can turn it off.
    • Today's four: Blackout, Night Bridge and Scramble get dailies like Dad's Safe. Everyone gets the same board, and each one has a spoiler-free result to share.
    • Blackout gets three new wirings (Cross, Wraparound and Dimmers) and Blitz: sixty seconds, as many houses as you can.
    • Crack Dad's Safe: every dad's safe holds his own loot for your shelf. There's a Safecracker mode with no notes on the door, and 4-digit safes once you've cracked three.
    • Scramble: swipe the tiles, and unlock four more pictures (a mixtape, a boombox, Dad's first car and a game pad).
    • Sound: little clicks, slides, splashes and a safe that clunks open. There's a switch on every Hands-on screen.
    • Eight new badges, from Three-Star Electrician to Grandpa Got Schooled.
  4. Version 1.69 · November 2026

    The shuffle button says what it does

    "Turns out people don't tap gears just because they're there."

    • The corner prop now wears a little shuffle badge, the same kind as Dad's music and voice bubbles, so it's clear you can tap it.
    • After a shuffle, Dad just says the new look's name, and his music cloud shows the new tune. For a few seconds the badge turns into Undo.
    • The Classic gear is a little bolder, so it no longer looks like background.
  5. Version 1.68 · November 2026

    Shuffle that keeps the music going

    "I didn't stop the music. I was just building suspense."

    • The music no longer cuts out after a shuffle, especially in the Android app. If your phone pauses it, it comes straight back.
    • A shuffle now picks a look, its own dad and its own tune together, so no more Gym Class with holiday music.
    • In dark mode, shuffle only picks dark looks.
    • The big pop-up is now a slim bar above the message box with one button: Undo. Tap the corner again for another shuffle.
  6. Version 1.67 · November 2026

    Better curls, better beard

    "My barber says sorry about last time."

    • Curls now keep each dad's own haircut and just make it curly, so nothing sticks out.
    • The beard stays inside his face, leaves his smile clear and comes with a moustache.
  7. Version 1.66 · November 2026

    Hair, beards and bouncier moves

    "Same dad, new barber."

    • The looks menu has new Hair and Beard rows under Skin tone. Hair can be as drawn, bald, grey at the temples or curly, and any dad can grow a beard. Each choice goes with every dad.
    • The moves that already moved Dad's head (the shimmy, the cape flap, the hat tip, the cool nod, the nap and more) now lean in before they start and wobble to a stop instead of snapping back.
  8. Version 1.65 · November 2026

    Smoother moves

    "I've been practising. Don't make it weird."

    • Dad's props now wind up, overshoot and settle instead of just fading in: mugs, books, lanterns, chalk, beakers and the rest pop in with a bit of bounce.
    • He reacts to what he's holding. His head dips and leans toward the prop, tips back when he sips, and follows the snowball when he throws it.
    • Hops squash before they jump and land with a little bounce. Waves wind down instead of stopping dead.
  9. Version 1.64 · November 2026

    Dad has habits now

    "I don't fidget. I'm expressing myself."

    • Each dad has his own way of standing around. Skater Dad sways, Video Store Dad leans, Bucket Hat Dad rocks on his heels, Lab Dad keeps glancing about, Coach Dad gives quick nods and Robo-Grandpa slowly nods off, then jolts awake.
    • Breathing is deeper and his head follows his chest, so he looks alive even at header size.
    • Talking is smoother, and when you type only his head tilts to listen, not the whole picture.
    • His moves play on top of his habits instead of fighting them. Reduce motion in your phone settings turns the habits off.
  10. Version 1.63 · November 2026

    Every dad has his own face

    "You can tell us apart now. I'm the handsome one."

    • The dads used to share one pair of eyes and one smile. Now each has his own: Arcade Dad's wide-eyed grin, Video Store Dad's half-asleep smirk, Filmi Hero Dad's moustache and big brows, Bucket Hat Dad's crinkled-eye laugh, and more.
    • Diner Dad's shades are see-through, so you can see his eyes. Count Dadcula looks a lot less cross.
    • Dad raises his brows while you type, pulls a thinking face while he works on an answer, and beams when you unlock something.
  11. Version 1.62 · November 2026

    Brain games pay tickets

    "Turns out thinking is worth a bouncy ball."

    • Puzzle Run, Today's puzzle, Dad's Safe, Escape Dad's Garage, Vocab Duel and the Grammar rounds all pay Prize Counter tickets now.
    • Tickets come from finishing, not from grinding: a Puzzle Run pays for each stop you clear plus a bonus for finishing, a Vocab Duel win pays more against tougher dads, and a Grammar round pays more for a better score.
    • The same daily limit as the other cabinets applies, so nothing can be farmed.
  12. Version 1.61 · November 2026

    Pick a skin tone for your dad

    "Same dad. Same jokes. New paint job."

    • The Me tab's looks menu has a new Skin tone row under Pick your dad: Steel, the robot he has always been, or one of four tones.
    • One choice goes with every dad, in the chat, in chess, in Tile Talk and on the pictures you share.
    • In the dark looks every dad now has dark eyes and a dark smile, Steel included, so he stays friendly at night. Steel also gets a lighter face plate there.
  13. Version 1.60 · November 2026

    A board to think on

    "I solve everything with sticky notes. It's a management thing."

    • The seating puzzle, the group-chat speed puzzle and the height puzzle now come with a thinking board. Drag the names into the slots, or tap a name and then a slot.
    • The board glows green when the order is right. It never scores anything, so you can still pick your answer however you like.
  14. Version 1.59 · November 2026

    Inside the garage

    "I found my other glove. It was in the other glove box."

    • Every room in Escape Dad's Garage is drawn now: the workbench, the toolbox, the old car and the mystery shelf. Each puzzle is an object in the room. Tap the one that's glowing to start it, and a sticky note with the digit appears when you've cracked the room.
    • When you crack the code, the garage door actually rolls up.
    • The Lost Cartridge: Chapter 1 is a real chalkboard with a chalk tray and a tap-to-flip letter decoder for the A=1, B=2 part. Chapter 2's note is an airmail letter now.
  15. Version 1.58 · November 2026

    Grammar you can drag and text

    "I texted 'k' to your mother once. We don't talk about it."

    • Tense Trouble is a text thread now. The situation arrives as a message, you tap the reply you'd send, and they answer back.
    • Simile, Metaphor or Analogy is a sorting game. The sentence rides in on a belt and you drag it into the right bin, or just tap a bin.
  16. Version 1.57 · November 2026

    Puzzle Run is a real run

    "A run with a start, a finish and a boss. Unlike my last jog."

    • Puzzle Run is five stops now: three puzzles, a hands-on game in the middle, and a boss puzzle at the end worth double points.
    • You have three hearts. A wrong answer costs one, and so does peeking at the answer. Finish with hearts left and you get a bonus and a result card.
    • Every puzzle sits in its own little scene: a voicemail from Dad for riddles, his case board for logic, his calculator for number puzzles and a pattern strip for patterns.
    • Today's puzzle is a numbered case now. Solve it and it gets a CASE CLOSED stamp.
  17. Version 1.56 · November 2026

    Vocab Duel is a real duel

    "I've lost to a lot of things. A dictionary is a new one."

    • You and the dad now face off with hearts and a health bar. Every right answer lands a hit, and a fast one is a critical hit.
    • Miss three times and the duel is over early. Land six hits and the dad starts to wobble. You still win with six, and eight out of eight is still a flawless win.
    • Each dad reacts in his own voice, from Skater Dad's "Clean landing!" to Robo-Grandpa's "In my day that would've earned a medal."
  18. Version 1.55 · November 2026

    Boss music

    "In my day the final boss came with an orchestra. Mine fits in a chip."

    • The secret chess boss now plays "In the Hall of the Mountain King" in 8-bit. It starts as a tiptoe and gets louder and faster every time round, just like the original from 1875.
    • Gamer Dad's chess match has two drift-phonk beats now, cowbells, heavy bass and rolling hats, and they take turns from one game to the next.
    • Tile Talk dads no longer borrow from the Radio. Each has a tune of his own: lo-fi for Modern Dad, drum and bass for Skater Dad, neon synth-pop for Arcade Dad, a trap beat for Gamer Dad, a drumline for Coach Dad, a busy little computer for Lab Dad, Bach on the harpsichord for Professor Dad, and a crackly old ragtime record for a certain grandpa.
    • All of it is made live on your device from scratch, with no recordings and no samples, so there is still nothing to download.

    "I can time your homework, your pasta and your push-ups. I cannot make any of them shorter."
